Private school cleaning · Brisbane

School cleaning that works around the school year.

We clean nine private schools across Brisbane. Daily cleaning keeps term running. The holidays are when floors get stripped and sealed, carpets steam cleaned and tiles scrubbed.

The school year

Daily work during term. Deep work in the holidays.

The heavy floor work needs empty buildings, so we plan it into the breaks. Private schools set their own term dates, so we work from your calendar.

  1. Term 1 Late Jan – Mar Daily clean: classrooms, washrooms, staff areas, halls
  2. Easter break April Vinyl strip & seal, carpet steam clean, tile scrub
  3. Term 2 Apr – Jun Daily clean: classrooms, washrooms, staff areas, halls
  4. Winter break Late Jun – Jul Vinyl strip & seal, carpet steam clean, tile scrub
  5. Term 3 Jul – Sep Daily clean: classrooms, washrooms, staff areas, halls
  6. Spring break Late Sep – Oct Vinyl strip & seal, carpet steam clean, tile scrub
  7. Term 4 Oct – early Dec Daily clean: classrooms, washrooms, staff areas, halls
  8. Summer break Dec – Jan The big one: whole-campus floor program, carpets and high glass

Across the campus

Every part of the school, not just the classrooms.

  • Classrooms & learning spaces

    Floors, desks, sills, whiteboards and bins, cleaned after the last bell.

  • Toilet blocks & change rooms

    Daily washroom cleaning with biological agents to deal with the cause of odours.

  • Staff rooms & administration

    Kitchens, offices, reception and meeting rooms, kept presentable for staff and visiting parents.

  • Halls, gyms & corridors

    High-traffic floors scrubbed during term, then stripped and resealed during the holidays.

  • Glass & entries

    Entry doors, partitions and windows, so the front of the school looks right at pick-up.

  • Outdoor areas

    Pressure cleaning for walkways, courts, covered areas and entries.

Washrooms

Toilet blocks that don’t smell like toilet blocks.

Odour is the most common complaint about washrooms in any busy building, and school toilet blocks are some of the busiest. Fragrance only covers it up for a while.

We use biological cleaning agents, which break down the residue that causes the smell. The product we use is biodegradable and works on most washroom surfaces.

It doesn’t replace everything. Toilet bowls still get a dedicated bowl cleaner, because that is what works best on them.

  • Breaks down the cause of odours
  • Biodegradable
  • Safe on most washroom surfaces
