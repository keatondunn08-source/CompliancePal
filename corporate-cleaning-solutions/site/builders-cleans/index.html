Builders’ cleans · Brisbane

From site dust to handover, in three stages.

Builders’ cleans are one of our three specialties. We clear the cement dust, paint marks and rubbish the trades leave behind, and get the building ready to hand over.

Book a site walk Call 0410 689 872

The three stages

Tell us the handover date. We’ll plan back from it.

  1. 1

    When the trades finish

    Rough clean

    Site rubbish out. Cement dust and debris off floors, sills, benches and stairs.

  2. 2

    Before inspection

    Final clean

    Glass inside and out, frames and tracks, fixtures, joinery, washrooms and kitchens, then floors finished to suit the surface.

  3. 3

    After defects

    Touch-up clean

    A pass over everywhere the trades went back into, so the handover walk-through sees a finished building.

What’s covered

Everything the handover inspection will look at.

  • Floors

    Cement dust, grout haze, paint and plaster spots lifted, then each floor finished to suit its surface.

  • Glass & frames

    Labels, stickers and overspray removed. Frames, tracks and sills vacuumed and wiped.

  • Joinery & fixtures

    Cupboards inside and out, benchtops, tapware, light switches and fittings.

  • Bathrooms & kitchens

    Tiles, grout, screens, basins and appliances, detailed for inspection.

  • Outside

    Pressure cleaning for paths, driveways, entries and car parks.

  • Rubbish

    Site rubbish and packaging cleared so the building is empty and ready.

New school buildings

Building at a school? We already clean schools.

When a new block goes up on a working campus, the builders’ clean is only half the job. We clean schools every day, so we know what a classroom, a toilet block or a staff room needs to be ready for students on day one.

We introduced ourselves to Brisbane’s building industry as a platinum sponsor at an industry event in Newstead, and builders’ cleans have been one of our three specialties ever since.
