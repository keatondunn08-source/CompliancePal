Coastal work

Every town on this coast wears a house differently.

Byron Bay

  1. Byron Bay

    Salt air

    Salt gets into everything near the beach and cheap fixings rust out within a season. I use marine-grade materials on coastal jobs so a repair stays fixed.

  2. Tweed Heads

    Border rules

    Tweed Heads is in New South Wales, not Queensland. NSW building codes, NSW council regulations and NSW insurance requirements apply, and that’s what I work to.

  3. Murwillumbah

    Hinterland moisture

    Up the valley it’s damp, not salty. Older timber homes take on moisture, so repairs here need a careful look at what’s behind the paint.
